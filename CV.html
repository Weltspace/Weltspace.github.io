Locally Private Subgraph Counting via Noisy Adjacency Matrix and DP on Randomized Data 本地化差分隐私下的子图计数：噪声邻接矩阵与随机化数据上的差分隐私

First author|Under review at USENIX Security 2027|Preprint arXiv:2507.06508|Code open-sourced|Leader of National-Level Innovation Training Program (top 3/130) 第一作者｜在投 USENIX Security 2027｜预印本 arXiv:2507.06508｜代码已开源｜国家级大创项目负责人（结题 Top 3/130）｜研究方向：局部差分隐私、分布式图统计、理论算法分析
  • Proposed dual frameworks — Noisy Adjacency Matrix (NAM) and DPRD (Differential Privacy on Randomized Data): NAM reformulates edge-LDP subgraph counting as algebraic estimation over randomized matrices, and DPRDP resolves the privacy-composition proof when a later round consumes randomized outputs — giving strict edge-LDP without any trusted server or shuffler, which prior protocols could not achieve. 提出噪声邻接矩阵（NAM）与随机化数据上的差分隐私（DPRD）双框架：NAM 将 edge-LDP 下的子图计数转化为随机化矩阵上的代数估计问题；DPRD 解决了交互式协议中后轮输入依赖随机化输出时的隐私组合证明难题，从而在不依赖任何可信服务器或 Shuffler 的前提下给出严格 edge-LDP 保证——此前 Watershed 等代表性 edge-LDP 方法均需引入可信第三方完成随机置换，无法在纯去中心化场景下部署。
  • Designed one- and two-round estimators (TriOR, TriMTR, QuaTR, TriTR*) and proved their guarantees: the first exact closed-form MSE for the RR-based one-round triangle estimator class, and the first relative-error guarantee for private subgraph counting — error provably does not grow with graph size when the clustering coefficient and average degree are stable, removing the degradation that bounds prior protocols. 设计 TriOR、TriMTR、QuaTR、TriTR* 等一轮 / 二轮估计器并给出完整理论保证：给出 RR 型一轮三角计数类的首个精确闭式 MSE（而非仅依赖渐近近似），以及私有子图计数领域的首个相对误差理论保证——当聚类系数与平均度稳定时，误差可证明不随图规模增长，解决了既有协议中精度随图增大而退化的固有问题，为后续在真实图网络上做预算分配提供了可分析的理论基础。
  • Built the full experimental pipeline on real graphs (Facebook, CA-AstroPh) with privacy-budget sweeps and SOTA comparisons: at ε = 1, TriMTR and QuaTR cut relative error to 0.0198×–0.0066× and 0.1963×–0.4407× of the strongest baselines (up to ~150× more accurate), outperforming every evaluated edge-LDP and shuffle-model method; TriTR* also dominates TriTR at equal download cost. 实现覆盖多个真实图数据集（Facebook、CA-AstroPh 等）、隐私预算扫描与 SOTA 对比的完整实验流程：ε=1 时，TriMTR 与 QuaTR 的相对误差分别降至最强基线的 0.0198×–0.0066× 与 0.1963×–0.4407×（最高约 150 倍精度提升），优于所有被评估的 edge-LDP 与 shuffle 模型方法；TriTR* 在同等下载开销下同样优于 TriTR，并在多组预算下表现稳定。
